---
title: "Incorrect payment currency"
description: "Check that a wrong currency creates no credit."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof prepares a payment in the configured currency, then sends a
successful-looking event in another currency.

## Why it matters

An amount is not meaningful without its currency. Treating NGN 5,000 and USD
5,000 as the same value can corrupt balances and customer entitlements.

## What the application should do

Compare the event currency with the stored payment currency. Reject or ignore
the mismatch and create no successful credit.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when no mismatched credit is committed. A credit in the wrong
currency fails `INV-002` or `INV-004`.
