---
title: "Failed payment"
description: "Check that a failed collection creates no credit."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof sends a failed collection event. The official [collection.failed
event](https://docs.bachs.io/guides/webhooks/events/collection-failed) describes
a payment attempt that did not succeed.

## Why it matters

A failed attempt must not grant the customer a paid entitlement or add money to
the application ledger.

## What the application should do

Record the failure and keep the payment unpaid. If the customer tries again,
that later eligible payment should have its own carefully checked outcome.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when no successful credit is saved. Any credit fails `INV-004`.
