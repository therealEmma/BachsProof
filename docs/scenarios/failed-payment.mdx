---
title: "Failed payment"
description: "Prevent fulfilment when a collection attempt failed."
---

## In plain English

The collection attempt failed. A failed attempt must not be treated as a paid
order or wallet deposit.

## What PayProof does

It creates a documented-shaped `collection.failed` event with `status: "FAILED"`
and no eligible financial state. Delivery is sequential and normally accepted
by a handler that records the event without crediting.

## Expected result

Zero credits, checked by `INV-004`. In Bachs, a failed collection can leave the
checkout open for another attempt; this scenario does not model that retry.
