---
title: "Underpaid payment"
description: "Prevent fulfilment when only part of the expected amount arrives."
---

## In plain English

The provider reports that only part of the amount due arrived. The application
must not credit the full order before the shortfall is resolved.

## What PayProof does

It creates a documented-shaped `collection.underpaid` event containing
`amount_paid`, `amount_expected`, `amount_remaining`, `currency`, and
`status: "UNDERPAID"`.

## Expected result

Zero credits. `INV-004` is the primary guard. PayProof does not send a later
top-up success in this scenario; use a separate lifecycle test if your business
process supports one.
