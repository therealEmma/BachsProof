---
title: "Underpaid payment"
description: "Check that an underpaid collection creates no credit."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof sends the documented crypto partial-payment event with the amount
paid and the amount still due. The official [collection.underpaid event](https://docs.bachs.io/guides/webhooks/events/collection-underpaid)
is specific to a crypto payment; a short bank transfer does not produce this
event.

## Why it matters

Granting the full order value after receiving only part of the money creates a
financial loss for the application.

## What the application should do

Record the partial state if useful, but do not create the full successful
credit. Your business may later handle a completed payment separately.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when no incorrect successful credit exists. Any credit for the
underpaid event fails `INV-004` or the amount rules.
