---
title: "Underpaid payment"
description: "Prevent fulfilment when only part of the expected amount arrives."
---

## What is being tested?

This test sends a `collection.underpaid` event. The provider says that only part
of the expected amount arrived.

## Why does it matter?

An application must not credit the full ₦5,000 order when the customer has paid
only part of it. `amount_paid` describes what arrived; it is not permission to
fulfil the complete order.

## What BachsProof does

BachsProof creates an event with `amount_paid`, `amount_expected`,
`amount_remaining`, the currency, and status `UNDERPAID`. The fixture pays about
half of the configured amount and does not send a later top-up success.

Run it with:

```bash
pnpm exec bachsproof test --name "underpaid payment"
```

## What the application should do

Record the shortfall, ask for or wait for the remaining amount according to your
business rules, and do not create the full payment credit yet.

## Pass and failure

A financially verified pass has zero credits and normally returns 2xx. Any
credit fails `INV-004`. Test a later successful top-up as a separate lifecycle
case.
