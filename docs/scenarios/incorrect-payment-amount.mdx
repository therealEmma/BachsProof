---
title: "Incorrect payment amount"
description: "Prevent fulfilment when the event amount does not match the order."
---

## In plain English

Your order expects NGN 5,000, but the incoming successful-looking event says a
different amount. The application must not credit the order.

## What PayProof does

It creates a `collection.succeeded`-shaped event for the reference with an
amount one minor unit higher than the configured amount.

## Expected result

Zero credits. `INV-004` catches fulfilment of an ineligible event. Amount
matching is the application's responsibility; PayProof does not know your
order table.
