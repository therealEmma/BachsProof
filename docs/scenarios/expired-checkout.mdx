---
title: "Expired checkout"
description: "Prevent fulfilment when a checkout expires without collection."
---

## In plain English

An open checkout can expire. Expiry is not payment success.

## What PayProof does

It sends a `checkout.expired` envelope without a charge and marks the payment
ineligible.

## Expected result

Zero credits. `INV-004` detects an application that credits merely because it
saw a checkout reference. A later valid success is covered by
[delayed payment confirmation](/scenarios/delayed-payment-confirmation).
