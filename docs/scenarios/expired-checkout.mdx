---
title: "Expired checkout"
description: "Check that an expired checkout creates no credit."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof sends a checkout-expired event without a successful collection. The
official [checkout.expired event](https://docs.bachs.io/guides/webhooks/events/checkout-expired)
describes an open checkout that expired before completion.

## Why it matters

An expired checkout should not create money in your ledger. Recording a status
is useful; treating expiry as payment is not.

## What the application should do

Record the expiry if your application needs it, but do not create a successful
credit for the reference.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when the ledger has no credit. Any credit fails `INV-004`.
