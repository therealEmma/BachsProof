---
title: "Incomplete checkout"
description: "Check that a checkout without a collected payment creates no credit."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof sends a checkout-completed event without evidence that money was
collected. The official [checkout.completed event](https://docs.bachs.io/guides/webhooks/events/checkout-completed)
can describe a completed checkout even when a payment was not collected.

## Why it matters

A customer reaching a success page or completing checkout is not, by itself,
proof that your application may grant money or access.

## What the application should do

Keep the payment incomplete or pending and create no successful wallet credit.
The handler may record the event for audit purposes.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when no credit exists for the prepared reference. Any credit
fails `INV-004`.
