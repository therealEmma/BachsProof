---
title: "Incomplete checkout"
description: "Prevent fulfilment when checkout completed without a collected payment."
---

## In plain English

Finishing a checkout screen does not always mean money was collected. A
checkout with `no_payment_required` and no charge must not credit a wallet.

## What PayProof does

It sends a `checkout.completed` envelope with a completed checkout, a
non-paying status, and no charge object.

## Expected result

Zero credits. The relevant rule is `INV-004` (no unexpected credit). A callback
verifier is necessary to prove the application did not write a credit.

This fixture tests the documented distinction between checkout completion and
payment collection; it does not model every checkout mode.
