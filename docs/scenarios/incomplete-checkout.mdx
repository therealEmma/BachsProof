---
title: "Checkout completed without payment"
description: "Check that a checkout without a collected payment creates no credit."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof sends a documented `checkout.completed` event for a free payment
checkout: `payment_status` is `no_payment_required`, the amount is `0`, and no
charge exists. The official [checkout.completed event](https://docs.bachs.io/guides/webhooks/events/checkout-completed)
can describe a completed checkout even when no payment was collected.

## Why it matters

A customer reaching a success page or completing checkout is not, by itself,
proof that your application may grant money or access.

## What the application should do

Keep the application payment unpaid and create no successful wallet credit. The
handler may record the completed checkout for audit purposes, but it must not
treat the checkout itself as money received.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when no credit exists for the prepared reference. Any credit
fails `INV-004`.
