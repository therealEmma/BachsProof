---
title: "Concurrent unique payments"
description: "Check that different payments remain independent."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof calls `createPayment()` for more than one payment and sends their
successful events concurrently. Each event has a different application
reference.

## Why it matters

Concurrency should not make one customer's payment update another customer's
record. This catches shared mutable state, broad queries, and test helpers that
accidentally reuse a reference.

## What the application should do

Look up each payment by its own reference and commit one correct ledger entry
for each. Do not use a global "last payment" value or a wallet-wide total as the
identity of the payment being processed.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when every reference has exactly its own correct credit. Missing,
mixed, or duplicated records fail the financial invariants.
