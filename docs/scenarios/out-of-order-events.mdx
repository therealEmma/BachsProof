---
title: "Out-of-order events"
description: "Check that an older event cannot undo a newer outcome."
---

## What it tests

BachsProof sends a newer successful event and then an older non-fulfilling
event. Bachs notes in its [webhook guide](https://docs.bachs.io/guides/webhooks/overview)
that webhook events are not guaranteed to arrive in the order they happened.

## Why it matters

An older status must not undo a completed payment, remove a valid credit, or
move a payment back to a state that can be processed incorrectly.

## What the application should do

Compare the event's business timestamp or state version with the stored state.
Keep the newer completed result and handle the older event without reversing a
valid financial entry.

The newer `collection.succeeded` delivery must receive a successful `2xx`
acknowledgment. The older `checkout.expired` delivery may be acknowledged or
intentionally rejected with an allowed `400`, `409`, or `422`. BachsProof treats
these as separate delivery expectations and still verifies the final payment
and ledger records.

## Pass and fail

The test passes when the final records contain one correct credit and a
completed payment. A reversed status, missing credit, duplicate credit, or
incorrect total fails the financial checks.
