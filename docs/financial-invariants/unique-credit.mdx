---
title: "INV-001: Unique Credit"
description: "At most one posted credit may exist for one eligible payment."
---

## Meaning

One customer paid once, so the application's ledger should contain at most one
credit for that payment reference.

## Example

Expected:

```text
payment pay-123 → one credit of NGN 5,000
```

Failing observation:

```text
payment pay-123 → two credits of NGN 5,000
```

## How PayProof evaluates it

It checks `observed.creditCount > 1`. The usual causes are duplicate deliveries,
non-atomic check-then-insert logic, or an idempotency key that is not durable
or unique.

Investigate the delivery IDs, event IDs, database uniqueness constraint, and
transaction boundary. The reference wallet's vulnerable mode demonstrates this
exact violation.
