---
title: "INV-001: Unique Credit"
description: "At most one posted credit may exist for one eligible payment."
---

## What it means

One customer paid once, so the application's ledger should contain at most one
credit for that payment reference. For a ₦5,000 deposit, one `500_000`-kobo
credit passes.

```text
Passing: payment pay-123 -> one credit of NGN 5,000
Failing: payment pay-123 -> two credits of NGN 5,000
```

## How BachsProof evaluates it

`verifyPaymentState` records `INV-001` when `observed.creditCount > 1`.
It does not require a particular event ID column or ORM; it checks the count
your callback returned. A missing credit is not this violation; it is
`INV-003`.

Common causes are duplicate deliveries, non-atomic check-then-insert logic, or
an idempotency key that is not durable or unique. Inspect delivery IDs, event
IDs, the database uniqueness constraint, and the transaction boundary.

The reference wallet's vulnerable mode deliberately produces this violation.
