---
title: "INV-001: Unique Credit"
description: "At most one posted credit may exist for one eligible payment."
---

## What it means

One customer paid once, so the application's ledger should contain at most one
credit for that payment reference. For a NGN 5,000 deposit, one `500000`
minor-unit credit passes.

```text
Passing: payment pay-123 -> one credit of NGN 5,000
Failing: payment pay-123 -> two credits of NGN 5,000
```

## Why it matters in BachsProof

BachsProof sends duplicate deliveries and duplicate event types because an
application must protect the financial effect, not only the provider event ID.
It does not require a particular ORM; it checks the credit count returned by
your `verify()` callback. A missing credit is not this violation; it is
`INV-003`.

Common causes are duplicate deliveries, non-atomic check-then-insert logic, or
an application idempotency key that is not durable or unique. Inspect delivery
IDs, event IDs, the database uniqueness constraint, and the transaction boundary.
