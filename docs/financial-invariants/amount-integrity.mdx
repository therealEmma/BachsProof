---
title: "INV-002: Amount Integrity"
description: "A posted credit must match the expected amount and currency."
---

## Meaning

If the test expects NGN 5,000, a posted credit must be NGN 5,000. NGN 4,999,
NGN 5,001, or a different currency is incorrect.

## How PayProof evaluates it

When at least one credit exists, PayProof compares
`creditedAmountMinor` and `currency` with the expected state. Amounts are
integer minor units in the provider-neutral core.

Investigate the order amount, currency matching, decimal-to-minor conversion,
and whether fees were incorrectly included in the customer credit.
