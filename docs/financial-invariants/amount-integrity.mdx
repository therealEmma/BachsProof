---
title: "INV-002: Amount Integrity"
description: "A posted credit must match the expected amount and currency."
---

## What it means

If the test expects NGN 5,000, the observed aggregate should be `500000` minor
units and currency `NGN`. NGN 4,999, NGN 5,001, or a different currency is not
the same payment result.

```text
Passing: expected 500000 NGN; observed 500000 NGN; creditCount 1
Failing: expected 500000 NGN; observed 499900 NGN; creditCount 1
```

## Why it matters in BachsProof

When at least one credit exists, BachsProof compares
`observed.creditedAmountMinor` and `observed.currency` with the expected state.
Values are integers so a floating-point rounding error cannot hide a mismatch.
The rule checks the aggregate returned by `verify()`; it does not guess an
observed amount or currency from configuration.

Investigate order amount matching, currency checks, minor-unit conversion,
rounding, and whether fees were incorrectly included in the customer credit.
