---
title: "INV-005: Aggregate Integrity"
description: "The total credited amount must equal the expected amount."
---

## Meaning

The sum of all credits associated with one eligible payment must equal the
expected amount. This catches totals that are wrong even when the credit count
looks plausible.

## How PayProof evaluates it

When the expected credit count is satisfied, PayProof compares the observed
aggregate amount with `expected.amountMinor`.

Investigate duplicate partial writes, unit conversion, rounding, fee handling,
and whether credits from another payment were linked to the same reference.
