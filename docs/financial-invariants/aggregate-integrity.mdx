---
title: "INV-005: Aggregate Integrity"
description: "The total credited amount must equal the expected amount."
---

## What it means

The total credited amount associated with one eligible payment must equal the
expected amount. This catches a wrong total even when the credit count looks
plausible.

```text
Passing: expected count 1, amount 500000 NGN; observed count 1, amount 500000 NGN
Failing: expected count 1, amount 500000 NGN; observed count 1, amount 499900 NGN
```

## Why it matters in BachsProof

BachsProof uses the integer `creditedAmountMinor` returned by `verify()`. The
rule is evaluated when the expected credit count exists; a duplicate is
primarily `INV-001`, and a missing credit is `INV-003`.

Investigate duplicate partial writes, unit conversion, rounding, fee handling,
and whether credits from another payment were linked to the same reference.
