---
title: "INV-004: No Unexpected Credit"
description: "An ineligible payment must not create money."
---

## What it means

`INV-004` fails when an incomplete, expired, underpaid, failed, or mismatched
payment has any credit in the saved ledger.

## Why it matters in BachsProof

The negative scenarios protect against granting money or access before the
application has evidence of an eligible payment. BachsProof waits through its
bounded observation window; it does not treat an unfinished background job as
a successful negative result.

Investigate eligibility checks, event status handling, amount and currency
validation, and whether a background worker writes after the handler should
have rejected the event.
