---
title: "INV-003: Completion"
description: "An eligible payment must produce the required credit."
---

## What it means

`INV-003` fails when a successful eligible event is delivered but the saved
records contain fewer credits than required.

## Why it matters in BachsProof

An HTTP success response is not financial completion. BachsProof polls when
your application reports pending work, then checks whether the required credit
was actually committed. A timeout is a failed, unverified result.

Investigate the worker, transaction commit, payment lookup, and the query used
by `verify()` when this rule fails.
