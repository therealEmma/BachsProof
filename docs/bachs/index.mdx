---
title: "Bachs integration"
description: "Understand the first provider adapter and its documented boundaries."
---

Bachs is BachsProof's first supported provider. The adapter is deliberately
explicit: `bachsAdapter({ mode: "mock" })` creates local fixtures shaped like
the documented Bachs webhook envelopes.

## What the adapter does

- Creates provider-shaped checkout and collection events.
- Builds the 15 scenario plans used by the CLI.
- Serializes only the provider envelope sent over HTTP.
- Signs a raw test body with `signBachsWebhook` when requested.
- Parses decimal-string amounts exactly with `bigint` before converting to
  provider-neutral integer minor units.
- Normalizes eligibility for the core's expected-state comparison.

## What it does not do

- It does not create a Bachs checkout or call a Bachs API.
- It does not retrieve a live payment or prove settlement.
- It does not emulate provider retry timing, a real customer, or every Bachs
  event family.
- It does not replace your application's order matching, durable deduplication,
  or ledger transaction.

The reference wallet uses the official `@bachs/sdk` server-side webhook helper
when a secret is configured. That application boundary is separate from
BachsProof's local mock adapter. Use a Bachs sandbox separately for provider-side
evidence.
