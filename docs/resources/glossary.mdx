---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Plain-English terms used in BachsProof."
---

- **Credit:** A saved financial entry that gives the customer money.
- **Idempotency:** Handling the same event more than once without repeating its
  financial effect.
- **Ledger:** The durable financial entries used to explain a balance.
- **Minor unit:** The integer storage unit for money. The configured amount is
  always sent as an integer.
- **Pending:** The application has not finished relevant background work.
- **Scenario:** One named payment situation BachsProof sends to the webhook.
