---
title: "Limitations"
description: "What BachsProof can and cannot prove."
---

BachsProof verifies the financial state visible during its observation window.
It cannot prove that an incorrect credit will never appear after that window.

Ordinary concurrent delivery cannot guarantee every possible database race.
Provider settlement, live Bachs behavior, network retries outside the test
requests, and production infrastructure are outside the tool's claim.

An allowed HTTP rejection is not proof of the application's exact business
reason. A `400` may mean a wrong amount, an unsupported event, a malformed
payload, or another validation failure. BachsProof does not depend on an
application-specific error body or code. It reports that the response was
allowed for that delivery and relies on `verify()` plus the financial
invariants to decide whether the saved state is correct.

HTTP success is also not financial proof. A worker may acknowledge an event
with `202` before it finishes, and an application may return a server error
after committing its state. BachsProof reports delivery and financial outcomes
separately, and a timeout or missing evidence remains `UNVERIFIED`.
