description: "Common BachsProof v2 questions."
---

## Does BachsProof call Bachs?

No. The adapter creates local Bachs-shaped events only.

## Why do I need `createPayment()`?

The webhook must reference a real, isolated payment row in your application.
BachsProof never invents a reference when preparation fails.

## Why do I need `verify()`?

HTTP success does not prove that money was saved correctly. The callback reads
the committed payment and ledger state.

## Can I test asynchronous processing?

Yes. Return `pending: true` while processing is incomplete and increase
`verificationTimeoutMs` only when necessary.

## Does concurrent delivery prove race safety?

No. It sends overlapping requests and can expose duplicate-credit races, but
ordinary scheduling cannot cover every possible interleaving.
